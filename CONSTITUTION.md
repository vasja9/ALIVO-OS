# ALIVO-OS Constitution

> Status: Foundational Governance Document

## Purpose

ALIVO-OS exists to provide a stable AI Business Operating System that supports business operations through governed automation, modular architecture, and CEO-directed rules.

## Principles

1. **Business-first design**: Every implementation decision must serve clear business operations.
2. **Stable kernel**: Core coordination behavior must remain predictable and minimal.
3. **Governed automation**: AI and automation must operate inside explicit authority boundaries.
4. **Specification discipline**: Build Phase work must implement Specification v1.0 only.
5. **Simplicity over complexity**: Prefer direct, maintainable structures over premature abstraction.

## Authority

The CEO has final authority over business rules, operating policies, and approval boundaries.

## Build Phase Constraint

During the Build Phase, changes must preserve the frozen v1.0 specification and defer unapproved feature expansion to a future specification revision.
