## Requirements

### 1. Principle

Knowledge is the most valuable business asset.

The purpose of ALIVO-OS is not only to store information.

Its purpose is to preserve understanding.

### 2. Types of Knowledge

The system shall distinguish between:

- Permanent Knowledge
- Business Knowledge
- Project Knowledge
- Operational Knowledge
- Personal Knowledge
- Historical Knowledge

Each type has different importance and retention.

### 3. Business Memory

Business Memory stores:

- important decisions
- decision outcomes
- business rules
- successful strategies
- failed strategies
- lessons learned
- recurring patterns
- CEO preferences
- approved standards

Business Memory becomes smarter over time.

### 4. Knowledge Lifecycle

Knowledge may be:

- Created
- Updated
- Archived
- Deprecated
- Deleted (CEO approval required)

Knowledge is never silently removed.

### 5. Source Attribution

Every important knowledge item shall record:

- origin
- author
- creation date
- confidence level
- supporting evidence
- related decisions

### 6. Learning

The system may learn from:

- approved decisions
- project outcomes
- workflow history
- business performance
- CEO corrections

The system may never invent business knowledge.

### 7. Knowledge Protection

Business knowledge is protected.

No silent modifications.

No hidden learning.

Every important change must be traceable.

### 8. Knowledge Retrieval

The system shall retrieve knowledge based on:

- context
- projects
- history
- importance
- recency
- relationships

Retrieval should prioritise relevance over volume.

### 9. Knowledge Quality

Knowledge should be:

- accurate
- traceable
- current
- understandable
- actionable

Obsolete knowledge should be clearly identified.

### 10. Long-Term Vision

Business knowledge compounds.

The value of ALIVO-OS increases through accumulated experience.

The system should become the trusted memory of the business.

## Implementation Rules

Do not define implementation.

Define only the business model.
