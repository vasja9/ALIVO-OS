# ALIVO Pain-to-Value Standard

CEO approved: 2026-11-24. Status: LOCKED. Scope: ALIVO business package only; all languages, all channels, all manually or automatically produced marketing.

This extends the existing solution > convenience > experience hierarchy and clarity, certainty, time, connection and status needs. It does not replace them.

## Required brief before creation

1. Reader problem: What is the hardest relevant part of the audience's everyday life? Treat audience insights as hypotheses unless supported by evidence.
2. Daily impact: What does this cost in time, energy, confidence, freedom or connection?
3. Desired outcome: What would the reader like to regain? This is an aspiration, not a health promise.
4. Credible ALIVO value: What useful understanding or educational next step can we actually provide? Identify the primary customer need and a concrete benefit.
5. Reader opening: Choose a respectful question or recognizable situation. Lead with this and useful value; only then offer a book, reading sample or other relevant next step if appropriate.

## Manual release checklist

- Does the actual opening speak to the reader rather than the product?
- Is the daily impact recognizable and the benefit realistic and supported?
- Does the offer follow useful value, without pressure, invented diagnoses, shame, fear or guaranteed health results?
- Is the language natural for the target market and the channel?
- For Facebook, are external URLs reserved for comments?

Use this checklist for hand-made video scripts, pins, social posts, advertising, emails and website marketing. The same policy is exported to automated writers; missing required evidence is rejected where the policy validator is integrated (blog and Pinterest). Other channel generators must call the shared review before approval when implemented; the channel enum alone does not constitute a live integration. Human or semantic review of the actual message remains necessary; structural validation cannot establish the truth or ordering of claims.

Brand philosophy: Don't fight your body. Work with it.

Example opening: “Does the afternoon feel harder than it used to?” Offer a useful explanation of possible factors and a reading sample, not a promise to restore energy.
