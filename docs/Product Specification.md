## Requirements

### 1. Product Identity

ALIVO-OS Personal Edition is a Personal Operating System.

It is designed exclusively for a single user.

Its purpose is to improve decision quality, execution, knowledge management and business effectiveness.

### 2. Product Goals

The system shall:

- reduce cognitive load
- centralise business knowledge
- support strategic decision making
- automate repetitive operational work
- preserve business memory
- provide actionable insights
- increase long-term productivity

### 3. Functional Domains

The product shall include the following domains:

- Executive Dashboard
- Mission Planner
- Projects
- Knowledge Library
- Business Memory
- Workflow Engine
- AI Workforce
- Market Intelligence
- Content Studio
- Publishing
- WordPress Integration
- Pinterest Operations
- Analytics
- Automation
- Executive Briefing
- Personal Evolution
- System Administration

### 4. Core Principles

Every capability shall comply with the Founding Charter.

Every capability shall create measurable business value.

Every recommendation shall be explainable.

Every important conclusion shall be evidence-based.

### 5. Functional Boundaries

The product is not:

- team collaboration software
- ERP
- CRM
- project management software
- accounting software
- chat application
- general-purpose AI assistant

The product may integrate with such systems but does not replace them.

### 6. User Model

Supported users:

Exactly one.

The CEO.

No multi-user functionality shall be introduced unless explicitly approved by the CEO.

### 7. AI Responsibilities

AI may:

- analyse
- recommend
- organise
- prioritise
- automate approved work

AI may not:

- redefine business strategy
- override CEO decisions
- execute irreversible actions without approval

### 8. Success Criteria

The success of ALIVO-OS Personal Edition is measured by:

- better decisions
- higher consistency
- less cognitive load
- more time for deep work
- improved business execution
