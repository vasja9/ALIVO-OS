## Objective

Create the official Success Measurement Framework for ALIVO-OS Personal Edition.

## Purpose

Define how the effectiveness of ALIVO-OS Personal Edition shall be measured.

The system exists to improve business outcomes.

Therefore success must be measurable.

## Repository Location

/docs/Success Measurement Framework.md

## Requirements

### 1. Core Principle

The purpose of measurement is improvement.

Not reporting.

Not collecting statistics.

Measurements must support better decisions.

### 2. Primary Success Indicators

The system shall measure improvements in:

- Decision Quality
- Time Saved
- Execution Consistency
- Business Knowledge Growth
- Goal Completion
- Opportunity Capture
- Cognitive Load Reduction

### 3. Secondary Indicators

The system may also measure:

- Automation Success
- Workflow Completion
- AI Recommendation Acceptance
- Content Production
- Publishing Performance
- Learning Progress

### 4. Measurement Rules

Every metric shall answer:

- What is being measured?
- Why is it important?
- How is it calculated?
- How often is it updated?
- Who uses it?

### 5. KPI Quality

Every KPI must be:

- Relevant
- Understandable
- Actionable
- Traceable
- Consistent

KPIs without business value shall not be retained.

### 6. Trend Analysis

The system should prioritise trends over isolated values.

Long-term improvement is more important than short-term fluctuations.

### 7. Executive Focus

The Executive Dashboard should display only the metrics that support decision making.

Information overload shall be avoided.

### 8. Continuous Improvement

Measurements should improve over time.

Obsolete metrics may be retired with CEO approval.

### 9. Success Definition

ALIVO-OS Personal Edition is successful when it consistently enables:

- better decisions
- better execution
- less wasted time
- greater strategic clarity
- more accumulated business knowledge

## Implementation Rules

Do not define dashboards.

Do not define visualisations.

Do not define databases.

Define only the business measurement model.
