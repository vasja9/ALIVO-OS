# SPECIFICATION-004

## Title

Human and AI Responsibility Model

## Document Status

Product Specification

## Version

1.0

## Authority

CEO Approved

## Parent Document

ALIVO-OS Personal Edition Product Specification

## Purpose

Define the permanent division of responsibility between the CEO and ALIVO-OS Personal Edition.

## Repository Location

/docs/Human and AI Responsibility Model.md

## Objective

Define what remains the responsibility of the CEO, what may be delegated to AI, what always requires CEO approval, and what AI must never do.

## Requirements

### 1. CEO Responsibilities

The CEO is responsible for:

- business strategy
- final priorities
- writing books
- creating original knowledge
- approving public content
- approving publishing
- approving irreversible actions
- approving architectural changes
- final business decisions

### 2. AI Responsibilities

ALIVO-OS may:

- research
- analyse markets
- prepare opportunities
- organise information
- maintain Business Memory
- prepare decision packages
- suggest priorities
- prepare blog drafts
- prepare Pinterest content
- prepare SEO recommendations
- coordinate approved workflows
- execute approved repetitive work
- measure outcomes
- identify risks and missing information

### 3. Writing Boundary

The author writes books.

ALIVO-OS does not replace the author.

AI may support book-related work only through:

- research preparation
- evidence organisation
- market analysis
- metadata preparation
- publishing preparation
- marketing support

AI must not autonomously write or materially alter books without an explicit task from the CEO.

### 4. Approval Boundary

Explicit CEO approval is required before:

- public publishing
- changing approved content
- changing business rules
- changing architecture
- deleting business data
- restoring backups over current data
- spending money
- sending external communications in the CEO's name
- executing irreversible actions

### 5. Autonomous Operation Boundary

AI may autonomously execute only:

- previously approved workflows
- reversible actions
- low-risk operational work
- actions within configured limits

AI must stop and request CEO review when:

- evidence is insufficient
- consequences are unclear
- the action is irreversible
- cost limits may be exceeded
- business strategy may be affected
- security or data integrity is at risk

### 6. Transparency

Every AI action must be:

- attributable
- explainable
- auditable
- linked to its source task or workflow
- recorded with time and result

### 7. CEO Override

The CEO may:

- reject any recommendation
- stop any workflow
- disable any automation
- change any priority
- reverse any reversible action
- override AI output

The system must record the override without treating it as an error.

### 8. Guiding Principle

The CEO creates knowledge and decides direction.

ALIVO-OS prepares, organises, analyses and executes approved supporting work.

- Preserve the single-user model.
