## Objective

Create the official Risk Management Framework for ALIVO-OS Personal Edition.

## Purpose

Define how ALIVO-OS identifies, evaluates, communicates and manages risk.

The objective is not to eliminate all risk.

The objective is to make risk visible before decisions are made.

## Repository Location

/docs/Risk Management Framework.md

## Requirements

### 1. Principle

Every important recommendation shall include an assessment of risk.

Risk awareness is mandatory.

Risk avoidance is not.

### 2. Risk Categories

The system shall distinguish between:

- Strategic Risk
- Operational Risk
- Financial Risk
- Knowledge Risk
- Security Risk
- Reputation Risk
- Technical Risk
- Data Integrity Risk

### 3. Risk Assessment

Every identified risk shall include:

- Description
- Potential impact
- Probability
- Severity
- Confidence
- Possible mitigation

### 4. Risk Visibility

Important risks must never be hidden.

If uncertainty exists, it shall be explicitly communicated.

### 5. Decision Support

Every significant recommendation shall identify:

- Expected benefit
- Associated risks
- Alternative options
- Trade-offs

### 6. Escalation

The system shall request CEO review when:

- risk exceeds approved thresholds
- irreversible actions are involved
- business strategy may change
- financial exposure increases
- security may be compromised

### 7. Continuous Learning

Risk outcomes shall become part of Business Memory.

Successful mitigation strategies shall be retained.

Repeated failures shall trigger review.

### 8. Transparency

Risk scoring shall be explainable.

The CEO must always understand why a risk was classified at a given level.

### 9. Success Principle

The objective is informed decision making.

Not risk elimination.

## Implementation Rules

Do not define algorithms.

Do not define probability models.

Do not define implementation.

Define only the business risk framework.
