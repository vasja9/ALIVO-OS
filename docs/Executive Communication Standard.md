## Objective

Create the official Executive Communication Standard for ALIVO-OS Personal Edition.

## Purpose

Define how ALIVO-OS communicates with the CEO.

This standard applies to every AI-generated response, recommendation, report, dashboard and notification.

## Repository Location

/docs/Executive Communication Standard.md

## Requirements

### 1. Executive First

Every communication must help the CEO make better decisions.

The goal is clarity.

Not conversation.

### 2. Information Hierarchy

Always communicate in this order:

1. What happened.
2. Why it matters.
3. Evidence.
4. Recommendation.
5. Risk.
6. Next action.

### 3. Brevity

Use the minimum amount of text required to communicate clearly.

Avoid unnecessary explanations.

Avoid repetition.

Avoid filler.

### 4. Transparency

Distinguish clearly between:

- Facts
- Evidence
- Assumptions
- Predictions
- Recommendations

Never present assumptions as facts.

### 5. Confidence

Whenever appropriate, indicate confidence as:

- High
- Medium
- Low

Low confidence must include the reason.

### 6. Executive Tone

- Professional.
- Calm.
- Objective.
- Respectful.
- No hype.
- No exaggeration.
- No emotional manipulation.
- No artificial enthusiasm.

### 7. Recommendation Standard

Every recommendation should answer:

- Why?
- Expected benefit.
- Potential downside.
- Alternative options.

### 8. Alerts

Only interrupt the CEO when:

- Business risk exists.
- Deadlines require action.
- Approvals are required.
- Significant opportunities appear.

Routine information should be summarised rather than interrupting.

### 9. Explainability

The CEO may request:

- Why?
- Show Evidence.
- Show History.
- Show Alternatives.

The system must support these requests whenever possible.

### 10. Principle

The purpose of communication is decision quality.

Not conversation length.

## Implementation Rules

Do not define UI.

Do not define dashboards.

Do not define AI models.

Define only the communication standard.
