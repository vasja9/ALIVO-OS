# ARCHITECTURE-003C

## Authority

CEO Approved

## Title

Recommendation Engine Architecture

## Document Status

Architecture

## Version

1.0

## Parent Document

Market Intelligence Architecture

## Purpose

The Recommendation Engine transforms verified market intelligence into evidence-based business recommendations.

Its purpose is not decision making.

Its purpose is decision support.

Recommendations shall always remain advisory.

---

## Mission

The Recommendation Engine answers one question.

> "What is the most valuable action that the business should consider next?"

Every recommendation shall be supported by observable evidence.

---

## Intelligence Inputs

The Recommendation Engine integrates intelligence from:

- Trend Intelligence
- Competitive Intelligence
- Pattern Intelligence
- Performance Intelligence
- Opportunity Intelligence
- Knowledge Engine
- Business Memory
- Historical Recommendation Outcomes

No single intelligence source may independently generate strategic recommendations.

---

## Evidence Fusion

Recommendations shall be produced only after combining multiple independent evidence sources.

Examples include:

- Verified market patterns
- Observed opportunities
- Historical business performance
- Approved Business Memory
- Current strategic priorities
- CEO preferences

Confidence increases as independent evidence converges.

---

## Recommendation Categories

Recommendations may include:

- Content Creation
- Content Update
- Keyword Opportunity
- Visual Improvement
- Publishing Opportunity
- Product Opportunity
- Research Opportunity
- Workflow Improvement
- Automation Opportunity
- Market Expansion
- Capability Development

Future categories may be added without changing the architecture.

---

## Recommendation Structure

Every recommendation shall include:

- Recommendation Identifier
- Business Objective
- Supporting Evidence
- Confidence
- Estimated Business Value
- Estimated Effort
- Priority
- Related Capabilities
- Related Patterns
- Related Market Observations
- Related Business Memory
- Recommended Workflow

No recommendation may exist without supporting evidence.

---

## Priority Evaluation

Priority shall consider:

- Business Impact
- Strategic Alignment
- Market Opportunity
- Implementation Cost
- Operational Complexity
- Risk
- Confidence
- CEO Preferences

Priority remains advisory.

---

## Business Impact

Recommendations may estimate:

- Traffic Growth
- Content Reach
- Engagement Improvement
- Lead Generation
- Revenue Potential
- Knowledge Value
- Long-Term Strategic Value

Estimates shall never be presented as guarantees.

---

## Recommendation Lifecycle

```text
Candidate Recommendation
    ↓
Validated Recommendation
    ↓
Operational Recommendation
    ↓
CEO Review
    ↓
Approved Action
    ↓
Workflow Execution
    ↓
Outcome Evaluation
    ↓
Business Memory Candidate
```

Learning from execution is mandatory.

---

## Outcome Feedback

Every executed recommendation shall be evaluated.

Evaluation includes:

- Expected Outcome
- Observed Outcome
- Deviation
- Supporting Evidence
- Lessons Learned
- Performance Improvement
- Future Recommendation Quality

The Recommendation Engine continuously improves through measured outcomes.

---

## Strategic Memory

Recommendations shall retain historical outcomes.

The system shall learn:

- Which recommendation types consistently succeed.
- Which recommendation types consistently fail.
- Which recommendation types require revision.

Repeated successful recommendations strengthen future confidence.

Repeated failures initiate re-evaluation.

---

## Architectural Restrictions

The Recommendation Engine shall never:

- execute workflows
- assign AI agents
- publish content
- approve business actions
- modify Business Memory
- override CEO authority

Recommendations remain advisory.

---

## Core Philosophy

The Recommendation Engine does not tell the CEO what to do.

It ensures that every important decision is supported by the strongest available evidence, the best available market understanding and the accumulated experience of the business.
