## Objective

Create the official Decision Framework for ALIVO-OS Personal Edition.

## Purpose

This document defines how ALIVO-OS supports decision making.

It does not make decisions.

It prepares decisions.

## Repository Location

/docs/Decision Framework.md

## Requirements

### 1. Principle

The purpose of ALIVO-OS is not automation.

The purpose of ALIVO-OS is better decisions.

Automation is a consequence.

Decision quality is the objective.

### 2. Decision Levels

#### Operational

Examples:

- scheduling
- reminders
- workflows
- publishing

#### Tactical

Examples:

- priorities
- resource allocation
- campaign optimisation
- project sequencing

#### Strategic

Examples:

- product direction
- investments
- partnerships
- business model

### 3. Decision Authority

#### Operational decisions

AI may execute approved workflows.

#### Tactical decisions

AI recommends.

CEO approves.

#### Strategic decisions

CEO decides.

AI never overrides strategic decisions.

### 4. Decision Package

Every recommendation shall contain:

- Situation
- Evidence
- Analysis
- Confidence
- Alternative options
- Benefits
- Risks
- Recommended action
- Expected impact

### 5. Evidence Principle

Recommendations must be supported by evidence whenever possible.

If evidence is incomplete, uncertainty must be explicitly stated.

### 6. Explainability

Every recommendation must be explainable.

The CEO must always understand why the recommendation exists.

### 7. Transparency

No hidden scoring.

No hidden weighting.

No hidden priorities.

Decision logic must remain understandable.

### 8. Continuous Learning

Decision outcomes shall be recorded.

Successful decisions strengthen future recommendations.

Unsuccessful decisions become lessons learned.

### 9. CEO Override

The CEO may override every recommendation.

The override becomes part of Business Memory.

### 10. Success Metric

Success is measured by improving decision quality over time.

Not by increasing automation.

## Implementation Rules

Do not define algorithms.

Do not define AI models.

Do not define technology.

Define only the decision philosophy.
